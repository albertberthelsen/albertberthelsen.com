Background

I wanted a home online that says more than a CV can: what I have worked on, what I took from it and how to reach me. I also wanted to build it myself rather than fill in a template.

How it is built

  • No framework. Every page is plain HTML with one stylesheet and one small script, so the site loads fast and is easy to maintain.
  • Design. A dark, editorial look with Fraunces for headings and a monospace face for labels. It is built mobile-first and tested at phone and desktop widths.
  • The chart in the background. Drawn on a canvas from a seeded random walk, so it looks like a price chart and stays the same on every visit.
  • Shipping. Version-controlled with Git, hosted on Vercel and served on my own domain over HTTPS.

What I took from it

I built the site with an AI coding agent, directing the design, the content and each step from an empty folder to a live domain. It is the same approach that I use at Kora: with the right tools, someone who is not a full-time engineer can still ship real software, as long as they know what they want and check the result.
