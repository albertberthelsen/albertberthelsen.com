Background

I wanted to understand Golar LNG properly. The company turns gas into LNG on floating plants and leases them out on long contracts. Its fourth unit has no customer yet, so I built a tool around one question: where should it go?

What it does

  • Market. Live gas prices, and what Golar’s share of the LNG price on its Argentine contracts is worth today.
  • Map. Every floating LNG unit, plus places where gas is burned off near the coast, from World Bank satellite data.
  • Pitch. What a Golar unit would be worth to the owner of a gas field, at today’s prices.
  • News. Floating LNG deals and gas discoveries from the last 30 days.

Alongside the tool, I wrote a short case on where the fourth unit should go, built on my own model in Python.
